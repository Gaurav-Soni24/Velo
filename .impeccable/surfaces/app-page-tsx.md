# Landing page (Persuade)

Scope: app/page.tsx and components/landing/*. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: recruiters, faculty, curious builders. Action: tap a bib (live demo), run the simulator, read the paper, open the code. Proof: JETIR paper, LCIT first place, 11 backend tests, working simulator. Admin dashboard and rider pages are out of scope. Full direction document: docs/creative-direction.md. Unresolved: admin screenshots need a recapture in the new frame.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A card tap is a timing chip crossing checkpoints; the page is built from race kit (chest band, pinned bib, stage profile, photo finish). Refuses the SaaS split hero with a device mockup and rows of cards.
OWN-WORLD: Flat Velo Blue #2440F0 fields, Kit Ink #0B1236, Bib White #F6F6F1; a three-stripe ink/lime/white chest band as the structural line; Chip Lime #C6F432 only for live or active; Red Lantern #FF5B3A for missing. Archivo extended black uppercase display, Archivo body, Geist Mono for chip IDs and endpoints. 2px radii, pinned paper bibs as the only shadowed objects.
STORY: Visitor taps a bib, watches the tap cross six checkpoints to an unlocked bike, understands the system from the stage profile, trusts it from the palmarès and photo finish, then runs the simulator or opens the code.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed blue. Headline TAP IN. RIDE OUT. across the top at display scale; under it, left, one paragraph and a link to the simulator; right, two pinned race bibs as the tap buttons; across the bottom, the full-width chest band with six labelled checkpoints and the live result line.
FORM: Race kit and chip timing, position 6 on my re-rolled grounded list (velodrome, painted cycle lane, night ride, thermal ride ticket, enamel head badge, race kit and bib, lab notebook plate). seed:dda0c3a942d0b23f
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
